# Privacy Policy for Sortshot

**Effective Date:** November 16, 2026

Sortshot is built from the ground up with a strict privacy-first architecture:
**"Your screenshots, sorted and useful. Nothing leaves your phone."**

---

## 1. What Information We Access
- **Screenshots and Images on Your Device:** Sortshot requests photo library access solely to identify, organize, and perform optical character recognition (OCR) on screenshots saved locally to your device.
- **Biometric Data:** Sortshot uses your device's biometric authentication (Face ID or fingerprint) to secure the Private Vault and app lock. Biometric data is processed exclusively by your device operating system; Sortshot never accesses, stores, or transmits biometric credentials.

## 2. On-Device Processing Guarantee
- **No Uploads:** All text recognition, categorization, entity extraction (bank accounts, amounts, dates, addresses, codes), and duplicate analyses are performed 100% locally on your device.
- **No Sortshot Servers or Backend:** Sortshot operates without any backend servers, cloud databases, or custom user accounts. Your data never touches any Sortshot server.

## 3. Third-Party Services
Sortshot includes two industry-standard SDKs:
- **Google AdMob:** Provides advertisement display in the free tier of the app. Google AdMob may process device identifiers, general location, and advertising IDs in compliance with Google's Privacy Policy. Personalized advertising consent is managed via the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent form, which you can adjust at any time in Settings.
- **RevenueCat:** Manages in-app purchases and subscriptions for Sortshot Pro. RevenueCat processes anonymous app user identifiers and store purchase receipts to validate entitlement status.

## 4. Data Retention and Deletion
All screenshot indexes, OCR results, and private vault images reside strictly on your local device. Deleting a screenshot inside Sortshot removes it from your phone's photo library. If you uninstall Sortshot, all local application database records and vaulted items are deleted immediately with the app.
